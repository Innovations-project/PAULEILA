Keep a word jar

Every word you learn is a tiny tool. Try one of these today:

  • Write the word somewhere you will see it.
  • Use it once out loud, even if it feels fancy.
  • Teach it to someone younger than you.
  • Draw the word instead of writing it.

Bonus: words you use three times tend to stay.
